Everything you need to know about the $Midnight(NIGHT) airdrop

$Midnight(NIGHT) is the native utility token of the Midnight(NIGHT) NFT ecosystem. It powers governance, staking rewards, marketplace fees, and exclusive holder benefits across the entire platform.

Tokens are distributed on a rolling basis after verification. Most claims are processed within 24–48 hours. You'll see the $Midnight(NIGHT) appear in your wallet once the transaction is confirmed on-chain.

Airdrop tokens for Bronze and Silver tiers are fully unlocked at distribution. Gold tier has a 3-month linear vesting, and Diamond tier has 20% unlocked at TGE with 6-month vesting for the remainder.

Each eligible wallet address can claim once. Sybil detection is in place — any attempt to game the system using multiple wallets will result in all associated addresses being blacklisted.

We support MetaMask, WalletConnect, Phantom (Solana), Trust Wallet, Coinbase Wallet, and any EVM-compatible wallet. ENS names are also resolved automatically.
